2. Data minimization

MamaFunke is designed so that as little personal data as possible is transmitted to us. You do not need an account to use the app. We do not ask for your name, date of birth, gender, precise location, or access to your contacts or microphone.

Your selected needs, topics, favorites, appearance settings and reminder times are stored only locally on your device. We do not receive this information and do not use it to create any server-side user or health profile.

3. Local app data

To provide the features you want, MamaFunke processes the following on your device in particular: the currently selected need, selected topics, saved favorites, display and appearance settings, reminder times and the onboarding status.

This data stays on your device. Any necessary access to device storage takes place pursuant to § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). You can change settings and favorites in the app. On Android, you can remove all locally stored data by clearing the app data in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app. On iPhone and iPad, you can remove it by deleting MamaFunke from your device. Uninstalling the app does not end an existing subscription on Google Play or the App Store.

If you choose your own background photo, the app asks for access to your photo library. The photo is stored only locally and is not transmitted.

5. Fonts and content

All thoughts, images and fonts are included in the app. The app does not download any of these later and does not connect to third parties for them.

6. Reminders and widget

Reminders are local notifications. No server-side push service is used and no device token is transmitted to us. Reminder times and status stay on your device. You can turn off reminders in the app and the notification permission in your Android or iOS settings at any time.

The widget on your home screen shows locally selected thoughts. Without Plus, the small widget remains available. Larger widgets fall back to the free view and never go blank.

9. Anonymous usage statistics, only with your consent

Without your consent, no usage analysis takes place. You can turn on “Anonymous statistics” during setup or in the settings. Only then does the app transmit individual usage events to TelemetryDeck GmbH, Von-der-Tann-Str. 54, 86159 Augsburg, Germany.

The data transmitted is the name of an event (for example “Plus page shown” or “Setup completed”), brief attributes of that event (for example, from where the Plus page was opened or which plan was chosen), a randomly generated ID for this installation and a random session ID. For technical reasons, your IP address is transmitted to TelemetryDeck in the process; we do not receive it. Your name, email address, needs, topics, favorites, the thoughts you read and health information are not transmitted.

We only evaluate the events in aggregate form to understand which parts of the app help and where it falls short. The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by turning off the switch in the settings. TelemetryDeck processes the data on our behalf on the basis of a data processing agreement.

More information: telemetrydeck.com/privacy

We store personal data only for as long as necessary for the respective purpose. After that, it is deleted or anonymized unless statutory retention obligations apply. We do not store app data that is kept only locally on your device.
